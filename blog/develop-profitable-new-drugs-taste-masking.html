How to Develop Profitable New Drugs: The BPS Advanced Taste-Masking Advantage

Traditional pharmaceutical manufacturing often involves energy-intensive thermal phases, particularly during wet granulation and particle coating processes. These processes not only drastically increase operational expenditure (OpEx), but also pose a critical risk to thermosensitive Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs).

For pharmaceutical companies seeking to launch profitable new drugs, optimizing production processes and driving down technological overhead are critical success factors.

The BPS Advantage: Cold Processing and Direct Mixing

BPSynergies' patented technology in most cases eliminates the need for heat-driven drying cycles or hot melt extrusion processes. Through an innovative Direct Mixing technology at room temperature, components are simply sieved and blended in standard equipment (such as a V-blender). This results in significant energy savings, immediate industrial scalability, and guarantees the chemical integrity of the most sensitive molecules, offering high-performance orodispersible powders and tablets.

A Revolution in Taste-Masking Efficiency

Historically, to mask the extreme bitterness of many APIs (such as analgesics, triptans, or antihistamines), the industry has relied on microencapsulation or particle coating with complex polymers (such as Eudragit). These methods, while effective, require highly specialized equipment, multiple production phases, and high energy consumption. This ultimately drives up the final product cost and undermines financial viability in generic or highly regulated markets.

The BPS taste-masking platform transforms this economic reality. Instead of creating costly physical barriers, BPS utilizes a proprietary synergistic technology that modulates the micro-pH in the oral cavity, effectively blocking bitterness perception at the taste receptors without the need for complex processing. The operational advantages are clear:

  • Decreased CapEx and OpEx: Completely eliminates costs associated with heating, solvent usage, lyophilization (freeze-drying), or spray drying.
  • Accelerated Production Speed: The direct dry blending process drastically reduces processing times, enabling a seamless and highly optimized technology transfer.

The Key to Profitable Value-Added Medicines

Developing successful new drugs relies on finding the perfect balance between high added value for the patient and optimized manufacturing. Formats like orodispersible tablets (ODTs) or stick packs greatly improve therapeutic adherence in patients with swallowing difficulties (dysphagia), offering a premium consumer experience without the need for water.

Thanks to the highly efficient operational profile of the BPS platform, pharmaceutical companies can launch Value-Added Medicines (VAMs) that maintain extremely competitive profit margins, even in markets where reference pricing systems force lower retail prices—thereby avoiding the commercial pitfalls often associated with expensive microencapsulation technologies.

Driving Sustainability in the Pharmaceutical Industry

By reducing the carbon footprint of the production line (through the absolute elimination of water, heat, and solvents), our technologies help pharmaceutical companies meet their growing Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and ESG goals. Simultaneously, they boost bottom-line financial performance through reduced processing times and clean, streamlined manufacturing.

With BPSynergies, patient-centric innovation and industrial profitability go hand in hand.
