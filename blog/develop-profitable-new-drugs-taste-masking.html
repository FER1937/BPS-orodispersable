How to Develop Profitable New Drugs: The BPS Advanced Taste-Masking Advantage

Traditional pharmaceutical manufacturing often involves energy-intensive thermal phases, particularly during wet granulation and particle coating processes. These processes not only drastically increase operational expenditure (OpEx), but also pose a critical risk to thermosensitive Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs).

The BPS Advantage: Cold Processing and Direct Mixing

BPSynergies' patented technology eliminates the need for heat-driven drying cycles or hot melt extrusion processes. Through Direct Mixing at room temperature, components are sieved and blended in standard equipment (such as a V-blender), guaranteeing the chemical integrity of sensitive molecules while producing high-performance orodispersible powders and tablets.

  • Decreased CapEx and OpEx: Eliminates costs associated with heating, solvent usage, lyophilization (freeze-drying), or spray drying (~30% total cost reduction).
  • Accelerated Production Speed: Direct dry blending drastically reduces processing times, enabling seamless GMP technology transfer.

The Key to Profitable Value-Added Medicines (VAMs)

Thanks to the efficient operational profile of the BPS platform, pharmaceutical companies can launch Value-Added Medicines in Stick Packs and ODTs that maintain competitive profit margins even in reference-priced markets.
