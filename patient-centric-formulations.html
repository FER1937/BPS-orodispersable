Patient-Centric Formulation Development & Therapeutic Adherence

A medication, regardless of its molecular sophistication, yields no clinical efficacy if sensory rejection or swallowing difficulties prevent the patient from taking it. BPS designs bespoke waterless oral formulations under Quality by Design principles for pediatric, geriatric, and dysphagia populations.
