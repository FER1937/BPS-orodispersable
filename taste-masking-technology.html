OBJECTIVE QUANTIFICATION

Electronic Tongue Electrochemical Fingerprinting

To provide pharmaceutical partners with reproducible, audit-ready analytical data, BPS quantifies Taste-Masking Efficiency (TME) using lipid/polymer membrane potentiometric sensors coupled with Principal Component Analysis (PCA).

The Euclidean distance shift on the PCA map objectively demonstrates the transition of the dissolved formulation from the aversive/bitter cluster of the pure API to the neutral/palatable cluster prior to human organoleptic panel confirmation.
