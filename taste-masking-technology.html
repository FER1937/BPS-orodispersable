Overcoming API Bitterness Without Polymeric Barriers

Conventional taste-masking relies on physical encapsulation (fluid-bed polymer coating, microencapsulation, or hot-melt extrusion) that encapsulates the API particle. While effective at delaying taste perception, physical barriers frequently retard drug dissolution and complicate bioequivalence.

Non-Occlusive Ternary Synergy

Our patented ternary system modulates the localized oral micro-pH and elevates the bitter taste detection threshold at the TAS2R receptor level immediately upon salivary contact—without coating the API crystal.

Unimpeded Dissolution Profile

Because the active ingredient remains free of hydrophobic or pH-dependent polymeric shells, intrinsic dissolution kinetics (Tmax and Cmax) are preserved—critical for immediate-release rescue therapies and bioequivalence studies.

Dual In-Vitro / In-Vivo Validation

Every formulation is quantified via potentiometric Electronic Tongue (E-Tongue) Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to measure Taste-Masking Efficiency (TME), followed by human organoleptic panels.
