DUAL-VALIDATION PROTOCOL

In-Vitro E-Tongue & In-Vivo Human Panels

1. In-Vitro Electronic Tongue (E-Tongue): To eliminate subjective bias and generate reproducible data, BPS utilizes an array of non-specific, cross-selective electrochemical sensors composed of lipid/polymer membranes that mimic human taste buds. When immersed in the dissolved formulation sample, sensors generate an electric potential fingerprint analyzed via Principal Component Analysis (PCA), mathematically confirming the shift from the "Bitter/Aversive" cluster to the "Neutral/Pleasant" cluster.

2. In-Vivo Human Organoleptic Panels: Standardized testing with healthy volunteers evaluates four critical parameters: Initial Taste Impact (immediate sensation upon dissolution), Mouthfeel & Texture (ensuring no grittiness with particle size <200 µm), Aftertaste (monitoring for lingering bitterness or metallic notes up to 5 minutes post-ingestion), and Throat Sensation (evaluating trigeminal burning or irritation).
