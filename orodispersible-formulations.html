The BPS proprietary masking process primarily utilizes a direct mixing of the components, a method that operates entirely at room temperature. By avoiding the application of heat or high-shear forces, we eliminate the risk of forming degradation products or impurities. This approach is specifically designed to enhance the stability of thermolabile active ingredients that are susceptible to thermal degradation.

In contrast to more invasive techniques—such as spray drying, hot melt extrusion (HME), or ion-exchange resin complexation—our process preserves the molecular integrity of the API while reducing total manufacturing costs by approximately 30%.

Versatile Formats Built for Seamless Tech Transfer

BPS formulations exhibit an optimized Hausner Ratio (1.04–1.14) and a Compressibility Index of 9–12%, ensuring precise dosing on high-speed lines and batch-to-batch reproducibility during storage. Components are sieved and blended in standard equipment (such as a V-blender) at room temperature across three commercial delivery systems:

  • 1. Stick Pack Powder (Protected under Core Claims & Claim 13): Direct-to-mouth orodispersible powder with instant melt-in-mouth technology and pre-measured unit-dose precision. Validated in industrial scale-up trials for taste-masked Paracetamol stick packs.
  • 2. Orodispersible Tablets – ODT (Protected under Claim 14): Fast-dissolving compressed tablets produced via direct compression of the core ternary powder, disintegrating rapidly in saliva without water.
  • 3. Chewable Tablets (Protected under Claim 14): Soft-textured, high-load capacity compressed tablets with immediate release profiles and receptor-level bitterness neutralization.
