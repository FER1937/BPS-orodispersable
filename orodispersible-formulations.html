SEAMLESS GMP TECH TRANSFER

Engineered for Standard GMP Manufacturing Lines

Formulators often face severe scale-up bottlenecks when transitioning spray-dried dispersions or fluid-bed coated microgranules to commercial manufacturing. Segregation, electrostatic charging, and poor die filling compromise dose uniformity.

BPS formulations are designed using the SeDeM expert system to ensure optimal bulk density, inter-particle cohesion, and lubricity—allowing direct blending in standard V-blenders or bin blenders and high-speed dosing without specialized CapEx.
